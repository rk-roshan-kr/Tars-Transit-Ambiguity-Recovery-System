## 2. Key Evidence & Statistics to Extract
- Question addressed: can exoplanet signal recovery ambiguity be isolated as a distinct, measurable quantity during vetting?
- Methods: the Recovery Ambiguity Index (RAI), univariate logistic model, and independent validation on $N=175$ TESS light curves.
- Principal findings: RAI-only model achieves comparable ranking performance to complex 16-feature stacks (AUROC = 0.6621), is highly calibrated (ECE = 0.0646), and legacy features are redundant (CMI p-value >= 0.20).
- Scope bounds: evaluated TESS Blind Validation Partition.
- Significance: parsimonious vetting, reducing dimensionality, and saving computing follow-up hours.
